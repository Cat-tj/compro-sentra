Send a support ticket

It doesn't have to be complete. Send what you have and we'll ask about the rest.

The ticket is sent as a WhatsApp message to our support team.

Prepare if available:

  • Product or deviceThe app, feature, or device with the problem.
  • LocationThe building or site where the problem occurs.
  • What you expectedWhat should have happened.
  • What happenedDescribe it, and include photos or screenshots if you have them.
The flow

After you contact us

  1. Send a message

    Via WhatsApp, email, or the ticket form.

  2. We review

    The team reads and checks the problem, remotely first.

  3. We resolve

    A remote fix, or the field team comes on site when needed.

  4. Done

    You receive an update on the outcome and notes.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help remotely?

Yes. We check most app issues and device settings remotely first. If needed, the field team comes on site.

How fast is support response?

SLA packages are available with response times of 1–4 hours depending on the tier.

Do you serve sites outside East Kalimantan?

Yes. Our projects span many provinces, with a mix of on-site and remote teams.

Is there a maintenance contract?

Yes. Visit frequency and coverage are tailored to the number of devices and your needs.
