Frequently asked questions

Which Starlink kit suits a mine?

For a fixed site office, usually Starlink Enterprise or Standard. For locations with strong wind, heat, and vibration, or for vehicle mounting, we recommend Starlink Performance.

Can it be installed where there is no grid power?

Yes. We size solar panels, batteries, and an inverter to the kit's power draw and hours of use.

Can the WiFi reach several buildings?

Yes, with additional routers and access points plus cable or radio links between buildings. We calculate coverage during the survey.

What about the monthly subscription?

Starlink bills the subscription according to the chosen plan. We help with plan selection, activation, and account setup.

Which areas do you serve?

Our field team is based in Samarinda and serves East Kalimantan. Other locations in Kalimantan and Indonesia can be discussed per project.

Ready to connect your site?

Tell us the location, number of users, and available power. The first discussion is free.
