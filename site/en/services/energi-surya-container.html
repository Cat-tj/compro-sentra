Solar and battery containers for sites that need their own power.

Sentra is an official partner of PT GIDA International Trading Indonesia and supplies GD-MES: a container that combines solar panels, battery storage and controls in one movable package. Designed for commercial and industrial use, including sites without grid power.

Free consultationSee model options

Problems we see in the field

  • Sites with no grid power or an unstable gridCamps, projects and remote areas depend on diesel generators.
  • Generator fuel costs keep recurringDiesel, delivery and engine maintenance pile up month after month.
  • Power cuts disrupt operationsServers, CCTV, networks and key equipment need backup power.
  • Installing panels and batteries separately is complexFoundations, placement and integration often eat time on site.

Frequently asked questions

Can it run without grid power?

According to the manufacturer's brochure, the system can run fully independently (off-grid). Suitability for your load must be calculated from the load list and hours of use.

How long does installation take?

The brochure says no complex foundation is needed. Real time depends on site and model, and is confirmed in the quotation.

How much does it cost?

Price depends on configuration and quantity, and follows the manufacturer's official quotation. Contact Sentra to request one.

Can it run heavy equipment?

It depends on inverter power and battery capacity. Peak load and starting current must be calculated before choosing a model.

What about warranty?

Warranty and after-sales follow the manufacturer's terms and are stated in the quotation.

Need independent power for your site?

Tell us the electrical load, hours of use and location. The first discussion is free.
