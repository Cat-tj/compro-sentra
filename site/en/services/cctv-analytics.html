Frequently asked questions

Do we need to replace our CCTV cameras?

Not always. The AI layer runs on top of existing cameras as long as image quality and positioning are adequate. Where coverage falls short, we recommend adding only what is needed.

Does video have to be sent to the cloud?

No. Analysis can run on a local server (on-premise), so video never leaves your site.

How long until it can be used?

A pilot in priority areas can usually start within 2–4 weeks, then expand once the results are measured.
