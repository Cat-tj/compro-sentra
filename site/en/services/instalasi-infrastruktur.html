Problems we often see

  • Unlabeled cablesWhen something breaks, nobody knows which cable goes where.
  • Networks that grew without a planSwitches and access points get added one at a time until the network is slow and hard to maintain.
  • Hot, crowded server roomsPacked racks, poor airflow, and equipment that goes down when the power is unstable.

What we do

LAN & WiFi networks

Structured cabling, switches, and access points designed around your floor plan and user count.

Fiber optics

Cable pulling, splicing, and link testing between buildings or sites.

Racks & server rooms

Server racks, cable management, and airflow for server rooms and small data centers.

Device power

UPS, grounding, and dedicated power lines so equipment stays up when the power is unstable.

CCTV & security

VIGI cameras, NVRs, and solar panels for sites without mains power.

Displays & videotrons

Indoor and outdoor videotrons, interactive flat panels, and service kiosks.

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you cover?

Our installation team covers East Kalimantan, including Samarinda, Balikpapan, Bontang, Tanah Grogot, and Penajam/IKN. Other locations can be discussed per project.

What do we receive when the installation is done?

Cable labels, route drawings, test results, and a list of installed equipment, so your team or another technician can maintain it.

Can you install equipment we already bought?

Yes. We can also supply equipment from official partners such as TP-Link, VIGI, HaganeRack, and HDE.
